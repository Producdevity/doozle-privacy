Privacy notice

Your sleep journal lives on your phone. Here is what that means.

About this notice

Droozle is provided by Producdevity. This notice covers version 0.1.0, a local sleep journal for adult parents and caregivers. Contact us about privacy at y.gherbi.dev@gmail.com. Effective date: 11 October 2026.

What stays on your device

You choose what to record: child nicknames, optional birth information, timezones, sleep times, awake intervals and notes. Droozle uses these records to show your journal and recorded totals. The journal, selected child, language and appearance preferences stay in the app’s private storage on your device.

This version needs no account and has no cloud synchronization. It does not send your journal to Producdevity or a Droozle server. It contains no advertising, app analytics or developer-operated crash reporting. We do not sell your journal information.

Exports, backups and platform services

You can export records as CSV or JSON. Your device’s share sheet lets you choose where to save or send the file. That destination has its own privacy practices. An exported copy remains there until you remove it; Droozle cannot delete it for you.

Android app backup is disabled in this build. Other device services and backups depend on your operating system and its settings. Keep a separate export if you need an independent copy; this app does not promise a recoverable backup.

If you send feedback through a store’s testing tools, the platform may share the diagnostics, screenshots and feedback you provide with the developer under its own terms. Please use fictional child information in feedback and screenshots.

Keeping and removing your journal

Your local journal stays on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Removing a sleep from the visible journal keeps an internal removal record for local consistency. Archiving a child preserves their history. These actions do not immediately erase every database byte.

To remove the full local journal, clear Droozle’s app storage in Android settings or delete the app from your iPhone. Export first if you want a copy. Remove any separate exports and operating-system backups yourself. We cannot read or remotely erase the journal on your phone.

If you contact us

When you email Producdevity, we receive your address and the information you send through our email provider, Google. We use it to answer and resolve your request, based on our legitimate interest in providing support. Please do not send sensitive child information or a full journal unless specifically needed for your request.

Support messages are kept only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, or to meet a legal obligation. You can ask to access, correct or delete correspondence, or object to its use, by emailing y.gherbi.dev@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection authority with a complaint.

Future versions

If we add hosted accounts, synchronization, analytics or payments, we will update this notice and the store declarations before enabling those features. The date above identifies the current notice.
